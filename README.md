# Mathematics workbenches

The index of our research workbenches: which repository holds which field, and the permanent page of every work.

**Every result here was produced by AI (GPT-6 Astra in ChatGPT Pro mode, and Claude; each record names the model, its effort and the date), and has been checked only by AI unless a record names a human who checked it.**

## By field

| Field | Repository | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Geometric complexity theory | [geometric-complexity-theory-workbench](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/geometric-complexity-theory-workbench) | Permanent against determinant, obstructions, plethysm and Kronecker coefficients, border rank, nonstandard quantum groups and their canonical bases. |
| Algebraic cycles and motives | [algebraic-cycles-and-motives-workbench](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/algebraic-cycles-and-motives-workbench) | The Hodge, Tate and standard conjectures, weights and purity, correspondences, periods, motives and their Galois groups. |
| Zeta and L-functions | [zeta-and-l-functions-workbench](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/zeta-and-l-functions-workbench) | Zeros, trace formulas, Artin L-functions, and what the Riemann hypothesis and its generalizations give, in arithmetic and in algorithms. |
| Schemes and moduli | [schemes-and-moduli-workbench](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/schemes-and-moduli-workbench) | Foundations of schemes, descent, étale, de Rham and crystalline cohomology, formal and p-divisible groups, Picard schemes, abelian varieties and their moduli. |
| K-theory and Galois groups | [k-theory-and-galois-groups-workbench](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/k-theory-and-galois-groups-workbench) | K-groups of rings of integers, Iwasawa theory, fundamental groups, anabelian geometry, Grothendieck-Teichmüller theory. |
| Homotopy and categories | [homotopy-and-categories-workbench](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/homotopy-and-categories-workbench) | Models for homotopy types, stacks, derivators, derived functors and homotopical algebra. |
| Algebraic groups and representations | [algebraic-groups-and-representations-workbench](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/algebraic-groups-and-representations-workbench) | Algebraic groups and Lie algebras, quadratic forms, lambda-rings, representation rings. |
| Combinatorial geometry and topology | `combinatorial-geometry-and-topology-workbench` (opens with its first checked work) | Polyhedra and maps, arrangements, stratified spaces, tame topology, the geometry of forms. |

- [collatz-workbench](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/collatz-workbench): The Collatz problem
- [erdos-straus-workbench](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/erdos-straus-workbench): The Erdős-Straus conjecture
- [erdos-straus-foundation](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/erdos-straus-foundation): The Erdős-Straus conjecture: sources and foundations
